What is ATLO?

Assembly, Test, and Launch Operations — the discipline of turning an intended change into something a business can actually depend on: fully assembled and bounded, proven under conditions harsher than production, and signed off by a role that never touched the build. Propel runs your roadmap the same way — three specialist functions below, each with a hard boundary, each escalating a real blocker instead of quietly working around it, so the fix that ships to your financial and technical roadmap is one that actually holds.
